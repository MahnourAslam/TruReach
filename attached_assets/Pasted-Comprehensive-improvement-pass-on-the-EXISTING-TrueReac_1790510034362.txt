CONTEXT (use this to guide copy):
TrueReach uses Oriane — whose core ability is "watch a video like a human, but with AI" — to do two things brands can't do today:
(1) Automate the manual check a person does by hand (or skips on big campaigns): did the creator actually show the product, say the brand, include the code/CTA?
(2) Reveal delivery metrics nobody currently calculates because no human could at scale: when the product first appeared, when the brand was first spoken, how many distinct times the logo showed, the exposure pattern across the video.
Performance metrics (views, reach, sales) are already tracked by other tools. "Delivery" — what the creator actually put in the video — has never been checked automatically. TrueReach outputs an evidence report to INFORM payment review; it never calculates or sets the creator's fee.
