metrics measured after video is posted by oriane:::


What Oriane checks
After the creator submits the TikTok, Oriane looks at the actual video.
It could answer things like:
Product
Did the product appear?
How long was it visible?
Did it appear clearly?
Brand
Was the brand/logo visible?
Was the brand name spoken?
Was the brand name written on screen?
Message
Did the creator say the required message?
Was the required discount code shown?
Was the required call-to-action shown?
Timing
Did the product appear within the first 5 seconds?
Was the video between 30–60 seconds?

Oriane isn't supposed to replace these metrics.
Your product could eventually have something like:
Contract requirement: Product must be clearly visible for ≥5 seconds + brand verbally mentioned + CTA included
Oriane: ✅ Product visible 7.2 sec | ✅ Brand mention | ✅ CTA detected
TikTok analytics: 182K views | 14.2K likes | 840 shares | 6.8K avg watch time
Brand tracking: 412 clicks | 87 code uses | AED 9,200 attributed sales
Existing system:
"How did the TikTok perform?"
Oriane:
"Did the TikTok actually contain what the creator agreed to make?"
