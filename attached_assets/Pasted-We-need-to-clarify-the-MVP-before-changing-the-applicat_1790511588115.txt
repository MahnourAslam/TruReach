PRODUCT DEFINITION

Creator Sponsorship Analyzer automates the manual review brands and agencies perform after a creator submits a sponsored video.

The product answers:

“Did the creator deliver the content requirements agreed with the brand, and where is the evidence in the video?”

This is different from conventional social-media analytics.

TikTok analytics answer:
“How did the video perform?”

Examples:
- views
- reach
- watch time
- likes
- comments
- shares
- saves
- clicks
- conversions
- sales

Our Oriane-powered analysis answers:
“What did the video actually contain?”

Examples:
- product appeared
- product visibility time
- first product appearance
- logo appeared
- brand name was spoken
- brand name appeared on screen
- required message was included
- discount code appeared
- CTA was included
- product demonstration occurred
- disclosure or permit number appeared
- video met timing and duration requirements

These new video-content metrics complement social-media performance metrics. They do not replace them.

MVP STRATEGY

For the hackathon, the product does not need to support every TikTok.

It must work accurately and consistently with 3–5 selected demo videos that we choose and test in advance.

Use a reliable uploaded-video or hosted-video fallback if TikTok embeds or downloads are unstable.

Accuracy and evidence are more important than broad URL support.

PROPOSED MVP WORKFLOW

1. The brand creates a simple delivery checklist before the video is submitted.
2. The creator submits a video or supported URL.
3. Oriane analyzes the actual video.
4. The application compares Oriane’s evidence against the checklist.
5. Every requirement receives one status:
   - Delivered
   - Not found
   - Needs review
6. The result includes timestamps, transcript evidence, visual evidence, and any estimated durations.
7. A reviewer can click an item to seek to the relevant video moment.
8. The application produces an auditable delivery summary for human approval.

EXAMPLE REQUIREMENTS

- Product must appear within the first 5 seconds.
- Product must remain clearly visible for at least 5 seconds.
- Creator must mention the brand verbally.
- Creator must include the required CTA.
- Discount code must appear on screen.
- Required ad disclosure or permit number must appear.

IMPORTANT ACCURACY RULES

Do not claim Oriane provides information that its API does not provide.

Before implementation:

- Inspect the real Oriane response for our selected videos.
- Save representative raw responses for development and testing.
- Identify which proposed checks Oriane supports directly.
- Identify which checks require inference.
- Identify which checks are currently unavailable.
- If Oriane returns matching frames rather than continuous visibility, group nearby frames into estimated visibility windows.
- Label inferred durations as estimates.
- Include confidence or “Needs review” when evidence is uncertain.
- Never fabricate a successful result.

We will manually annotate the selected demo videos with expected timestamps. This manual ground truth is only for validating accuracy during development. The finished product should automate the review.

SOCIAL-MEDIA METRICS

Do not make TikTok analytics integration a dependency for the MVP.

The interface may display mock or manually supplied performance metrics for context, but label them clearly.

Keep these groups separate:

1. Performance metrics
Views, reach, engagement, watch time, clicks, conversions, and revenue.

2. Oriane video metrics
Product, logo, speech, on-screen text, CTA, required messages, timing, visibility, and demonstrations.

3. Contract evaluation
Whether the detected video evidence satisfies each campaign requirement.

SCOPE LIMITS

Do not build:
- automated payments or escrow release
- creator fee recommendations
- legal compliance decisions
- automatic contract parsing
- authentication
- campaign-management dashboards
- support for every TikTok URL
- cross-campaign benchmarking

The application supports human review. It does not automatically approve payment or provide legal advice.
